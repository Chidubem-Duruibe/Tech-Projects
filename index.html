Who we are

Fluent in balance sheets and back-ends.

BTA exists for students who don't want to choose between the boardroom and the terminal. We build the skills, connections, and hands-on experience that sit at the intersection of both.

01 / MISSION

Bridge two worlds

We help members build fluency in both business strategy and the technical tools that now run every industry — from finance to product to operations.

02 / COMMUNITY

A network that lasts

Small enough that everyone's known by name, big enough to open doors — our members support each other well past graduation.

03 / EXPERIENCE

Learn by doing

Case competitions, live client projects, and technical workshops replace lecture slides with real, resume-ready work.
